# 05 — Visual Design Analysis

Observed from `screenshots/01-home-hero.png` and image alts (E-01, E-04).

- **Shape:** Rounded-rect pill floating ~8px above screen edge; optional full-width variant, top or bottom.
- **Segments:** Dock is divided into rounded sub-capsules (apps group, AI group, calendar, weather, clock) with subtle separators.
- **Density:** ~44–52px tall; two-line micro-typography (label + value) inside tiles; small progress bars.
- **Material:** Dark translucent glass; "Liquid Glass" mode distorts/refracts the wallpaper (not just blur).
- **Styles:** Black, White, Liquid Glass, iOS Glass, Midnight (E-07 §4).
- **Colour accents:** Green progress bars for usage, red/orange calendar date, blue weather tile.
- **Typography:** Clean sans (system/Inter-like), bold numerals for values.

## For our independent design (do NOT copy)
Our product must use its own name, icon, palette and layout. Decisions taken in `../README.md`:
monochrome "ink" surface with one accent colour, square-ish 12px radius modules, mono numerals,
Windows-native acrylic/mica backdrop instead of custom refraction.
